How does the site works

The data used on this site comes from every competitive scene the pro players plays on patch 7.41. The data from patch 7.40 and below are not used because the updates are significant enough to change the meta, affecting player's hero choices and performance.

This site helps to guide you on who to pick on the fantasy pick compendium event, especially for casual players who doesn't follows dota 2 competitive scene. Hope the information served will influence you to pick reasonable player, not some washed-up famous individual.
